Independent AI Analyst & Writer

I track the AI landscape full-time — reading every major model release announcement, benchmark paper, regulatory filing, and infrastructure shift that comes out each month, then synthesising it into a single authoritative report that professionals can actually use.

The AI Landscape was built out of frustration with coverage that is either too shallow (tweet-length takes) or too narrow (single-company focus). Each monthly report covers frontier models, open-weight developments, agentic tooling, infrastructure, policy, and field-specific implications — all in one place.

Before writing, I cross-reference primary sources: official release blogs, benchmark leaderboards, regulatory filings, and earnings calls. Every claim is cited. Nothing is paraphrased from other newsletters.

Editorial Standards

What This Publication Covers

Primary Sources Only

Every data point is traced to an official blog post, benchmark leaderboard, regulatory filing, or peer-reviewed paper. No second-hand paraphrasing.

Monthly Cadence

Published at the end of each calendar month. The goal is comprehensive coverage of the full 30-day window — not reactive takes on individual news events.

Cross-Disciplinary Scope

Each report covers frontier models, open-weight releases, agentic tooling, developer infrastructure, enterprise deployments, and global AI policy in a single document.

Field-Specific Analysis

Rather than covering AI in the abstract, reports are structured around who is actually affected — engineers, enterprise teams, creative professionals, finance, policy makers.
